The job brief

Senior enablement manager

This team needs someone to build self-serve customer onboarding, run the learning platform, and use evidence to improve the program.

The platform is Workday Learning. The team can train an experienced learning-platform owner on its tools.

What counts as evidence here?

Look for work the person owned: a program they built, systems they maintained, and how they checked whether the program helped. A result alone does not tell you how they achieved it.

All names, employers, and results in this exercise are fictional. These are practice rules for this job brief.

How this exercise works

Rule choices are checked against an authored answer key for this fictional job brief. Candidate evidence and feedback are scripted. No AI model evaluates your input, no match percentage is calculated, and your notes stay in this tab. This is a learning exercise, not a working applicant tracking system or a claim about a particular product.
