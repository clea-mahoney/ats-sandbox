Why I built this

I want customer education to help people use AI thoughtfully in their actual work. This lab connects live practice with the decisions a team makes inside a product, so time saved on review can become time spent getting to know candidates. This lab is based on a fictional recruiting platform, with assumptions made on how its AI can be configured.

    Use this in a live workshop

    Ask customers to make an individual decision before discussing it in pairs. Compare which source supports each claim, what remains unknown, and who owns the next action. Use the review as a follow-up resource.

    Then give them a fresh example in a test environment. Check whether they can revise a criterion, catch an unsupported claim, and draft a useful question. Afterward, partner with the recruiting team to review corrections, missed evidence, and time spent.
